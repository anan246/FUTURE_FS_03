# Client Pitch: L'Étoile Artisanal Cafe & Bistro Website

---

## 1. Business Profile
**Business Name:** L'Étoile Artisanal Cafe & Bistro  
**Business Type:** Premium Artisanal Bakery, Gourmet Cafe & Bistro  
**Location:** 42, 100 Feet Road, Indiranagar, Bengaluru, KA 560038  

L'Étoile is a premier local establishment specializing in authentic French sourdough baking (72-hour slow fermentation), specialty micro-lot coffees, and all-day brunch.

---

## 2. The Problem
Before this website project, local cafe customers faced common points of friction:
* **Lack of Visual Imagery:** High-end cafe customers want to *see* the ambiance, coffee craft, and pastries before visiting.
* **Inconvenient Reservations:** Customers had to call during peak hours or message via Instagram DMs to check table availability.
* **Cluttered Social Links:** Important information like operating hours, dietary options, and directions were fragmented across social media bios.
* **Mobile Inconvenience:** Lack of quick 1-tap WhatsApp and direct call triggers for mobile users on the move.

---

## 3. The Solution
We designed and engineered a custom **editorial, image-first local business website** tailored specifically for **L'Étoile Artisanal Cafe & Bistro**:

1. **Stunning Photography & Aesthetics:** High-resolution photography highlights the 84-layer butter croissants, pour-over coffee ritual, and courtyard dining.
2. **Instant Table Booking Engine:** Customers can select dates, times, guest sizes, and preferred seating (Indoor Bistro, Garden Patio, Bar Counter) with instant feedback and ticket confirmation.
3. **Interactive Menu with Filters:** Guests can easily filter by category (Pastries, Coffee, Brunch, Desserts) and dietary needs (Vegan, Gluten-Free, Chef's Choice).
4. **Fullscreen Image Lightbox Gallery:** An interactive 10+ image masonry gallery allowing customers to immerse themselves in the cafe's atmosphere with keyboard and gesture support.
5. **Mobile-First Quick Actions:** Floating 1-tap WhatsApp chat and direct call buttons for friction-free customer reach.
6. **Centralized Business Configuration (`business.ts`):** All store information, hours, menu prices, and images can be re-branded or updated in under 2 minutes from a single file.

---

## 4. Key Business Benefits & ROI
* **Higher Table Conversion:** Direct online table booking reduces bounce rate and fills off-peak dining slots.
* **Elevated Brand Prestige:** A bespoke, luxury visual design positions L'Étoile above generic competitor templates.
* **Local SEO Dominance:** Structured JSON-LD LocalBusiness metadata ensures high visibility on Google Maps and search results for "artisan bakery Indiranagar".
* **Time Savings:** Automated contact form and booking endpoints streamline staff operations.
